This release changes how bots talk in group chats: one answers at a time and hands the conversation on, and each group gets its own rules, pass limit and reply interval.

New
- Group chats pass the conversation along: one bot answers at a time, then hands it to whoever should speak next, or stops and waits for you. The one you @mention or quote goes first.
- Each group has its own rules, a limit on how many times the bots pass it on after each of your messages (8 by default, at least 2, or no limit), and a reply interval between bots. Set them from the phone app.
- Bots can quote a message they answer, narrate and send pictures in groups, and the phone app can read groups aloud in each bot's voice.
- The assistant can list, make, rename and delete your group chats, add or remove members and set their rules; it asks before deleting.
- Bots keep one life across their own chat and their groups, and a switch lets each one message you first.
- New bots use a DeepSeek model when one is available.

Fixed
- SVG style rules now apply in the Markdown preview.
- The side panel remembers where a file's tab was left.
- Deleting a bot also removes its conversations.
