This release rebuilds how the computer and the phone connect, and adds usage tracking and /btw side questions; diffs and split panes get a large round of work too. On the phone, this needs 0.1.5, and each phone pairs again once.

Phones
- The computer and the phone connect a new way: directly on the same network, through a direct connection made across networks otherwise, through a relay only when neither works, end-to-end encrypted. A computer whose IP changes is still reached.
- In Settings → Mobile, relays are picked automatically, or one is chosen, or your own; nothing to set on the phone.
- Settings → Mobile shows the phone app's download QR codes first, the same as on the download page; the pairing code, clicked, copies its link.
- On Android, the app now downloads its updates itself and installs them with a tap.

New
- Usage: each upstream's calls, their tokens and cost, in a dashboard in Settings, and each conversation's own usage page.
- /btw: ask something in a conversation of its own beside the one you're in; Esc goes back.
- Diffs side by side and inline, as in JetBrains IDEs, with an overview lane; each change can be undone where it is. Review follows each conversation's own edits.
- Split panes: a project's agents open in its own pane; what is stacked in a pane shows as tabs; each row and column resizes on its own.
- Terminals reorder by dragging, and the chat's panel and the side panel share them.
- ⌘L / Ctrl+L adds the code selected in the editor to the chat's input; queued messages can be dragged into another order.
- Type over the sidebar to search the projects; upstreams take custom models and endpoint URLs.

Improved
- While an agent works, the window redraws at most 30 times a second, using less power.
- Search honors .gitignore at every level, nested repositories included.

Fixed
- No white strip around the window on Windows.
- Stopping a background command no longer hangs.
- Each tab's editor is back where it was left; going to definitions and references works while language servers start.
