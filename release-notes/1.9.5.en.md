This release brings group chats with your bots, lets bots suggest what you might say next, and lets them color how they sound when read aloud.

New
- Group chats: put two or more bots in one chat with you. After you stop typing, they take turns deciding whether to answer, the ones you @mention first, and keep it to a few replies for each message of yours. Made and used from the phone app.
- Bots can suggest two or three short replies at the end of their message, when the phone app has suggestions turned on.
- Bots can mark how a line sounds ([whispers], [laughs]) when the phone app reads their messages aloud; the cues are left out of the chat and notifications.
