This release improves bots on the phone: they talk faster and more like people, with narration besides. It also fixes 1.7.2's bots running in the background without a phone. On the phone, this needs 0.1.4.

Bots
- A bot sends several messages in one reply, instead of a request to the model for each, so it answers faster and no longer repeats itself on weaker models.
- A bot can narrate what it does or how it looks, shown between its messages.
- When a bot speaks of one of your agents' conversations, it sends a card that opens it.
- A bot can come back to you in as little as 10 seconds, not a minute.
- On the phone, you can delete your chat with a bot to start again; who it is and what it remembers stay.
- The assistant introduces the other bots to you as its friends.
- Each bot has an Agent notifications switch, off at first, the assistant's too. On, it hears when any agent waits, fails, finishes or runs long; off, of none.

New
- In Settings → Customize, each skill, MCP server and server tool has a switch, followed at once by agents already running.

Fixed
- With no phone paired, bots are no longer told what your agents do, and do not come back to what they meant to. Each such notice had a bot answer on your model, paid for and read by no one. Once a phone is paired, they carry on.
- After BaoCode restarted, a bot sometimes went on in a new, empty conversation, its chat seemingly gone on the phone. It now goes on in its own.
- A bot's conversation no longer shows in the desktop's notifications, the Dock badge or the tray, and a notification no longer opens it as an ordinary conversation.
- A bot skips what happens to agents that its chat does not need, instead of answering each one.
- Built-in core: once a plan is put to you, the turn ends, instead of some models putting it again and again in the same turn.
