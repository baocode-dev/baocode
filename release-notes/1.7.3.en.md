Fixes 1.7.2's bots running in the background without a phone.

Fixed
- With no phone paired, bots are no longer told what your agents do, and do not come back to what they meant to. Each such notice had a bot answer on your model, paid for and read by no one. Once a phone is paired, they carry on.
- A bot's conversation no longer shows in the desktop's notifications, the Dock badge or the tray, and a notification no longer opens it as an ordinary conversation.
- Each bot has an Agent notifications switch, off at first, the assistant's too. On, it hears when any agent waits, fails, finishes or runs long; off, only of the agents it started or wrote to. On the phone, this needs 0.1.4.
- A bot skips what happens to agents that its chat does not need, instead of answering each one.
